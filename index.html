⠿ Shot filters & tools

Every shot of a player's season, on a 3D court

  1. Each marker is one real shot — green went in, red missed. Taller spikes mark areas where the player scores best. Drag to spin the court, scroll to zoom.
  2. Use “Shot filters & tools” (bottom-left) to switch players or show only certain shots — e.g. just three-pointers, or just makes.
  3. Use the “Camera view” buttons (bottom-right) to change angles — Top-Down looks like a classic paper shot chart.
