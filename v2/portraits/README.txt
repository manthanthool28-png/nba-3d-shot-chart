Optional player portraits for the "Portrait" player-card style.

Drop an image here named after the player, and it is used automatically:
  luka_doncic.jpg / .png / .webp
  stephen_curry.jpg
  (player name lowercased, accents stripped, spaces -> underscores)

Every portrait is drawn into the same frame, crop and background gradient,
so the card looks consistent whichever player is selected. Without a file,
a styled silhouette in the player's team colours is drawn instead.

IMPORTANT: no photos ship with this app. NBA headshots and most press
photos are copyrighted — only add images you have the rights to use.
